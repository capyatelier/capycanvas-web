## Place a ruler

Choose the **Ruler** tool, then pick a type in **Tool Set**. A **Straight** ruler guides strokes along one line. A **Parallel** ruler keeps every stroke going in the same direction wherever you draw, and a **Radial** ruler guides strokes toward a single point, like lines meeting at a vanishing point. Drag on the canvas to place the ruler.

The ruler is only a guide. It shows on screen but is never part of your exported image.

## Draw along it

Make sure **Snap to rulers** is turned on, then switch back to a pen or brush and draw near the ruler. Your stroke follows the guide, while the brush, pressure and color work just as they normally do. Turn snapping off whenever you need to draw freely again.

**Show rulers** hides and shows the rulers. A hidden ruler doesn't guide your strokes, so if snapping seems to have stopped working, check that the rulers are showing.

## Adjust or remove it

Choose the Ruler tool again to adjust a ruler. Drag the ruler to move it, or drag one of its ends to change its angle. **Delete ruler** removes the selected ruler, and anything you have already drawn stays where it is.

For a single clean line or shape, the [Figure tool](/docs/tools/figures/) may be quicker. To move lines you have already drawn, use [Moving and transforming](/docs/tools/transforms/).

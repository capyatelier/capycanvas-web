## Place a ruler

Choose **Ruler**, then **Straight**, **Parallel** or **Radial** in Tool Set. Drag to establish its position and direction. A straight ruler guides strokes along its line; a parallel ruler supplies a common direction, and a radial ruler guides lines toward its center.

Position the ruler where you need the constraint before switching back to a drawing tool. It is a guide, not an inked line in the exported image.

## Draw with snapping

Enable **Snap to rulers**, then choose a pen or brush and draw near the guide. Brush size, pressure and color still determine the mark. Turn snapping off when you need unrestricted strokes beside the ruled lines.

**Show rulers** controls their visibility. Hidden rulers do not guide strokes, so check both visibility and snapping if the expected constraint is missing.

## Revise the guide

Return to Ruler or use Operation to select and adjust a ruler. Move its body or an endpoint to change its position or direction. **Delete ruler** removes the selected guide while retaining the paint already drawn.

Use [Figure](/docs/tools/figures/) for a single geometric shape, or [transform](/docs/tools/transforms/) when you need to change existing painted lines.

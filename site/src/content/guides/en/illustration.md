## Prepare the document

Start with [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart/) if you have not checked your pen. Choose **Paint** so Tool Set, Color and Layers stay visible. Create a **1200 × 1200** document for a portrait like the example, or use your own drawing at a suitable size.

The example has a head, hair and a coat with a scarf. The named layers identify what you are editing; the workflow does not depend on tracing this exact character.

## Follow the four stages

Begin with [Sketching](/docs/illustration/draft/), then continue through Line art, Masking and Rendering in the same document. Keep Sketch and Color rough for reference, even after hiding them. Later stages add layers rather than replacing the previous work.

Each stage has a real editor capture. The numbered outlines point to the controls described in its caption. Open an image at full size to inspect the layer order and thumbnails.

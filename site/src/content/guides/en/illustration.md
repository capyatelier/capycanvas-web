## Before you start

Use [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart/) to check the available version and pen input if you have not done so. Keep the brush list and Layers panel visible; [Workspace and canvas](/docs/workspace/) explains how to place them around your drawing.

You will use undo, brush-size adjustment, and canvas navigation throughout the tutorial. Put those commands on keys or pen buttons you can reach while drawing, using [Input settings](/docs/advanced/input/). Choose whichever controls fit your device.

## Follow the stages in order

Begin with [Sketching](/docs/illustration/draft/) and keep the same document through all four stages. Each stage explains the tools it introduces, then uses them on the layers from the previous stage. Links within the steps lead to the relevant settings when you need more detail.

Use your own character drawing. The steps refer to hair, skin, and clothing so it is clear which region to edit; choose the pose and colors you want to work with.

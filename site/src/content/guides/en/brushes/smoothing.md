You can smooth a brush stroke while you draw. Smoothing settings are saved separately with each brush preset.

![The Smoothing group with Strength and Adjust by speed.](shot:brushes/smoothing-settings)

## Strength

**Strength** sets how strongly the stroke follows a smoothed path. At 0, smoothing is off. A higher value removes more small changes in direction and makes the mark follow the pen farther behind.

Brush smoothing Strength is separate from the brush's primary **Strength** setting.

![The same input path drawn twice, the upper stroke with smoothing off and the lower with a high smoothing Strength.](shot:brushes/smoothing-comparison)

## Adjust by speed

Turn on **Adjust by speed** to reduce smoothing during faster movements and apply more smoothing during slower movements. Turn it off to use the selected **Strength** throughout the stroke.

## Availability

The **Smoothing** group appears for freehand drawing and retouching tools that use a brush path. It is not available for tools whose path or shape is set by handles, points or a selection.

Use [Resetting brushes](/docs/brushes/reset/) to restore the preset's smoothing settings.

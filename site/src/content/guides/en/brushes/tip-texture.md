You can change the shape of each dab and how much paper grain shows in the
mark with the **Tip** and **Texture** settings in the **Tool** panel
([Size, opacity and flow](/docs/brushes/basics/)).

![The Tool panel for Pencil with the Tip group and the Texture group.](shot:brushes/tip-texture-settings)

## Tip

### Hardness

Sets how sharp the edge of each dab is. Lower values give a softer edge.
**Watercolor Wash** and **Wet Watercolor** have a ragged tip and no **Hardness**.

### Spacing

Sets the distance between dabs, as a percentage of the dab's diameter at the
current pressure. The slider goes up to 100%, and you can type values up to
1000%.

### Angle

Turns the tip, from −180° to 180°. A round tip looks the same at any angle.

### Size variation

Changes the size of each dab at random by up to this percentage either way. At
10%, dabs vary between 90% and 110% of the brush size.

### Rotation variation

Sets how far each dab turns at random, as a fraction of a full turn in either
direction.

## Tips that turn with the stroke

The tips of **Paintbrush**, **Textured Flat**, **Pastel Block**, **Transparent
Glaze**, **Opaque Gouache**, **Loaded Oil**, **Palette Knife**, **Watercolor
Wash**, **Wet Watercolor**, **Dual Texture** and **Natural Blender** follow the
direction of the stroke. **Angle** has no effect on these brushes.

**Dry Scumble** and **Realistic Brushed Ink** add the stroke direction to
**Angle**.

## Texture

**Texture strength** appears on the pencil and pastel brushes, **Paintbrush**,
**Bristle Paintbrush**, **Textured Flat**, **Dry Scumble**, **Transparent
Glaze**, **Opaque Gouache**, **Loaded Oil**, **Palette Knife**, **Dual
Texture** and **Natural Blender**.

Grain is fixed to the canvas. Each brush has its own grain, and you can't
change its size or choose another grain.

### Texture strength

Sets how much the paper grain shows in the mark. At 0% the grain has no effect,
and at 100% the paint follows the grain completely.

You can take a tool, a brush, the color wheel or the layers out of a toolbar as a bubble on the canvas.

## Taking a bubble

Drag a title bar button, a toolbar tool, or a medium or brush in a tool panel or drawer onto the canvas. A drop on a toolbar, a panel or the title bar, or within 80 px of the bar or panel it came from, takes nothing. Each workspace keeps its own bubbles.

| Drag out | You get | Menu items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A tool such as **Brush**, **Eraser**, **Sculpt** or **Select** | A tool bubble | The tool's settings, **Put back** |
| A medium or a brush | A medium or brush bubble | The brush's settings, **Put back** |
| **Color Picker** | A bubble with a **Brush size** arc and a **Strength** arc, and a readout of the value while you drag. Tap its center to start or stop the color picker | **Swap sliders**, **Put back** |
| **Undo** or **Redo** | One bubble. Tap the left half to undo and the right half to redo. A half is dimmed while there is nothing to undo or redo | **Put back** |
| **Brush color** | A color ring | **Recent colors**, **Palette**, **Primary and secondary**, **Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, **Put back** |
| **Layers panel** | A Layers card | **Simple**, **Full**, **Fold when not in use**, **Close**, **Put back** |

![A brush bubble with its settings halo, the bubble from Color Picker with a size readout, and the Undo and redo bubble.](shot:bubbles/halo-controls)

A tool has one bubble, and dragging the tool out again moves it. Commands such as **Save**, sliders, **Tool Options** and dividers can't become bubbles.

## Halo and chooser

A bubble comes out selected, with its settings halo open if the tool has settings. Each tap moves it through halo, chooser and closed, except that a brush bubble has no chooser. Tapping an unselected tool's bubble selects the tool first.

Drag along a halo arc to change a setting, and double-tap or double-click the arc to reset it. The chooser rings the tool's media around the bubble: select a medium to fan out its brushes, then select a brush.

Hold the bubble or right-click it to open its menu. Its check items put settings in the halo, up to six for each brush.

![A bubble's chooser with the Watercolor medium selected and its brushes beside it.](shot:bubbles/dial-chooser)

## Color ring

You can pick a color on the ring as in the [Color panel](/docs/color/color-panel/). A new ring is the wheel alone. Turn on **Recent colors**, **Palette** or **Primary and secondary** in its menu to add beads, and choose **Small**, **Medium** or **Large** for the size. Select a bead to use its color, and slide along a row of beads to scroll it. Select the palette bead to switch palettes or choose **Edit palettes…**. Pinch with two fingers to resize the ring.

![The color ring with its beads and menu.](shot:bubbles/color-ring)

## Layers card

The **Layers panel** bubble opens as a card. **Simple** shows each layer's eye, thumbnail and name, and **Full** shows the Layers drawer. Selecting anywhere else, or painting, folds the card to a square with the layer count; select the square to open it again. Turn off **Fold when not in use** to keep the card open until you choose **Close**.

![The Layers card in Simple.](shot:bubbles/layers-card)

## Moving and putting back

Drag the face of a bubble to move it, or the rim of the plate on the color ring. With touch, two fingers move a bubble from anywhere on it. Bubbles push each other aside.

To put a bubble back, do one of the following:

- Drop it on a toolbar, a panel or the title bar.
- Choose **Put back** in its menu.
- Choose **View > Put back all bubbles**.

**Window > Undo Layout Change** reverses taking, moving and putting back.

## Painting and Zen mode

A stroke near a bubble fades the bubble and passes under it. Painting closes a chooser and folds a Layers card. Bubbles stay on screen in [Zen mode](/docs/customize/zen/).

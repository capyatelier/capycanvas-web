You can hide the title bar and the docked panels and toolbars in Zen mode. The
canvas then fills the window.

![The canvas in Zen mode, with a floating panel and the Capy button in the corner.](shot:zen/zen-canvas)

## Entering and leaving Zen mode

Do one of the following:

- Choose **View > Zen mode**.
- Press **Tab**.
- Select the Capy button at the left end of the title bar. In Zen mode, the button is in the top-left corner of the window.
- Type "Zen mode" in [command search](/docs/start/command-search/).
- With the **Procreate Style** keymap, tap the canvas with four fingers.

Do the same again to leave Zen mode. The Capy button looks pressed while Zen mode
is on.

Zen mode is saved with each workspace and stays on after a restart. **Customize
Title Bar…** turns Zen mode off.

## What stays on screen

Zen mode also hides collapsed columns and the footer. These stay:

- floating panels
- the canvas action bar next to a selection or transform
- the Capy button, unless **Show Capy in Zen mode** is off
- notices

Keys and command search (**Ctrl+K**) work as usual.

## Showing the controls for a moment

The hidden controls come back while a menu or dialog is open and while you drag
or resize a panel. Press **Escape** to show them until the pointer moves.

With **Reveal panels near screen edges** turned on, they also come back while the
pointer is near the top edge, or near an edge with docked panels.

On a touchscreen, the first tap on a hidden control shows the controls, and the
second tap uses the control.

## Docking in Zen mode

While docked panels are hidden, a floating panel you drag can join only other
floating panels. With **Reveal panels near screen edges** on, dragging to an edge
with docked panels shows them as drop targets.

## Capy button menu

Right-click the Capy button, or hold it with a finger, to open its menu.
**Change icon…** opens Preferences at **Button icon**. In the title bar, the
menu also has **Customize Title Bar…**.

Holding the Capy button with a finger doesn't turn Zen mode on or off.

![The menu of the Capy button.](shot:zen/capy-menu)

## Zen mode preferences

Choose **Edit > Preferences**, select **Appearance**, and find the **Zen mode**
group. These settings apply to every workspace.

### Show Capy in Zen mode

Keeps the Capy button in the top-left corner in Zen mode. On by default. While
it is off, press **Tab** or use command search to leave Zen mode.

### Reveal panels near screen edges

Shows the hidden controls while the pointer or a finger is near a screen edge
with hidden controls. Off by default.

### Button icon

Sets the Capy button's picture: **Looking up** (the default), **Facing
forward**, **Bathing** or **Sleeping**. A **Zen mode** button in a toolbar uses
the same picture.

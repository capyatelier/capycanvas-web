Each workspace keeps its own toolbars and title bar. **Window > Undo Layout
Change** (**Ctrl+Alt+Z**) reverses the last change.

## Toolbar menus

To open a toolbar's menu, right-click the toolbar's background or its grip, or
hold it with a finger or pen. Right-click or hold a tool for the tool's menu.

![The toolbar menu with the tile sizes.](shot:toolbars/toolbar-menu)

## Showing and hiding toolbars

Do one of the following:

- Choose **Window > Quick Access Toolbars** and the toolbar's name. Toolbars on screen have a check mark.
- Choose **Hide *name* toolbar** from the toolbar's menu.
- Type the toolbar's name in [command search](/docs/start/command-search/).

A toolbar you show again opens at the top edge of the window.

After you delete the **Tools** or **Commands** toolbar, **Restore Tools** or
**Restore Commands** appears in the same submenu. It brings the toolbar back with
its starting tools.

![The Quick Access Toolbars submenu of the Window menu.](shot:toolbars/quick-access-menu)

## New toolbars

Do one of the following:

- Choose **Window > Quick Access Toolbars > New Toolbar…**. The toolbar opens at the top edge.
- Choose **New Toolbar…** from the **Panel Group** menu of a tab group. The toolbar becomes a tab in that group.

On the web and Android, type a **Toolbar name**, select the tools to add, and
select **Create Toolbar**. In the Linux, Windows, macOS and iPad apps, type a
name, choose **Empty toolbar** or a saved toolbar under **Start with**, and
select **Add to Workspace**.

Each toolbar needs a name of its own.

![The New Toolbar dialog with one tool selected.](shot:toolbars/new-toolbar-picker)

## Adding and removing tools

- To add tools at the end, choose **Add Tools…** from the toolbar's menu.
- To add tools before a tool, choose **Insert Tools…** from that tool's menu.
- To remove a tool, choose **Remove Tool** from its menu.
- To move a tool, hold it, then drag it within the toolbar or into another toolbar.

Besides commands and tools, the **Add Tools** list has brushes, brush sizes,
**Color Picker**, **Brush size slider**, **Brush opacity slider**, **Tool
Options** and **Divider**. It also has an entry for each panel, such as **Layers
panel**, that opens the panel in a drawer.

**Tool Options** shows the settings of the current tool across the rest of the
toolbar. Its menu has **Horizontal: Text**, **Horizontal: Icons** and **Show
Sliders**.

![A tool's menu with the Remove Tool and Insert Tools… items.](shot:toolbars/tool-menu)

## Tile sizes

Choose **Small Tiles**, **Medium Tiles**, **Large Tiles**, **Medium Labeled
Tiles** or **Large Labeled Tiles** from the toolbar's menu. Labeled tiles show
each tool's name. New toolbars use **Small Tiles**.

## Configuring a toolbar

Choose **Configure *name* toolbar…** from the toolbar's menu. The toolbar opens
enlarged, with the commands of its menu in a column beside it. Drag the buttons
of the enlarged toolbar to reorder them. **Escape** closes the column.

![The Tools toolbar with its configuration column.](shot:toolbars/configure)

## Floating toolbars

Double-click the grip of a floating toolbar to switch it between a grid, a
column and a row.

## Renaming and duplicating toolbars

Choose **Rename *name* toolbar…** or **Duplicate *name* toolbar…** from the
toolbar's menu. A duplicate has the tools and tile size of the original.

## Deleting toolbars

To delete a toolbar:

1. Choose **Window > Quick Access Toolbars > Manage Toolbars…**.
2. Select the toolbar.
3. Select **Delete Toolbar…**.
4. Select **Delete Toolbar** to confirm.

**Window > Undo Layout Change** brings back a deleted toolbar.

![The Manage Toolbars dialog with a toolbar selected.](shot:toolbars/manage)

## Toolbar Library

In the Linux, Windows, macOS and iPad apps, you can save a copy of a toolbar and
add it to other workspaces. Choose **Save to Toolbar Library…** from the
toolbar's menu. To add a saved toolbar, open **Manage Toolbars…**, go to
**Saved Toolbars**, select the toolbar, and select **Add to Workspace**.

## Customizing the title bar

Do one of the following:

- Choose **Window > Customize Title Bar…**.
- Right-click or hold the title bar, and choose **Customize Title Bar…**.

A bar opens under the title bar with the items you can add.

- To add an item, drag it into the title bar. Selecting an item in the bar does nothing.
- To move an item, drag it to a new place.
- To remove an item, drag it off the title bar, or select it and press **Delete**.

To add tools, drag **Add Tools…** into the title bar and choose the tools for
that place. The bar also has **Small**, **Medium** and **Large** for the size of
the title bar, and **Show footer** for the bar under the canvas with the zoom
and rotation.

Select **Done** to keep the changes or **Cancel** to discard them. Until then,
single-key shortcuts and command search don't work.

![The title bar while it is being customized.](shot:title-bar/editing)

## Title bar items

| Item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Capy (Zen Mode)** | The button for [Zen mode](/docs/customize/zen/) |
| **Main Menu** | One button with every menu (not on macOS) |
| **Menu Labels** | The menus as a row of names (not on macOS) |
| **Settings** | A button that opens Preferences |
| **Full Screen** | A full screen button (web only) |
| **Workspace Switcher** | A button for each workspace |
| **Document Title** | The drawing's name, or a tab for each open drawing |
| **Clock**, **Battery** | The time and the battery level |
| **Space** | An empty gap |
| Tools | Any item from the **Add Tools** list except the sliders and **Tool Options** |

On the web and in the Linux, Windows, macOS and iPad apps, **Clock** and
**Battery** appear only in full screen.

Each item except **Space** and tools can be placed once. Items that don't fit
move into a **…** menu.

## Reuse recent colors

Open the **Palettes** tab next to **Color**. The top row shows the colors you have recently painted with, newest first. A color is added only when you actually use it in the drawing, so the row doesn't fill up with colors you just looked at. Select the arrow at the end of the row to see more of your history.

Select any swatch to make it your painting color. In Sketch, the same swatches appear below the color wheel when you open the Color drawer.

## Build a palette

Below the recent colors is the current palette. Capy Canvas includes a few palettes to start with, and you can switch between them with the palette menu at the bottom of the panel. To add your current color, select the **+** tile at the end of the palette. You can drag swatches to put them in a different order.

To start a fresh palette, open the palette menu and choose **New Palette…**. The same menu can rename or remove a palette. Your palettes aren't stored inside your drawings, so they are available in every drawing you open.

## Import and export palettes

Choose **Import Palette…** in the palette menu to bring in a palette from another app. Capy Canvas can read palettes from Procreate, Clip Studio Paint, Photoshop, Krita, GIMP and Affinity. **Export Palette** saves the current palette in one of several formats, so you can take it back to another app or share it with someone else.

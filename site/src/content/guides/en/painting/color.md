## Choose the hue and its variation

In **Color**, drag around the outer ring to choose a hue, then use the inner field to adjust its variation. The available field shapes use different color coordinates; their readout identifies the active model. Choose the shape that makes the color easiest to select.

Keep a short test stroke beside the image. A color can look different against a white background, existing paint, or a partially transparent brush.

## Keep two colors available

Use the foreground and background swatches to choose which color to edit. Swap them when you need the alternate color. **Gradient** uses this pair, and a Figure with **Outline + fill** uses both colors.

The checkerboard swatch selects **Transparent paint**. A drawing tool then erases instead of adding the foreground color. Return to the foreground swatch before painting a new color.

## Sample from the document

Choose **Eyedropper** and select whether it reads the editing layer or the visible image in Tool Set. Sample the desired area, then return to your drawing tool. Sampling the visible image includes the appearance of the composed layers; the editing-layer option isolates that source.

For repeated color areas, keep them on [separate layers](/docs/layers/basics/) so later changes remain manageable.

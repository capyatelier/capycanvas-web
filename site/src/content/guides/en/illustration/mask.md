## Separate the main areas
Create flat-color layers below the ink for the subject, clothing, and background. Give them useful names and keep their edges easy to see.

## Define the shapes
Use selections, fill, and a brush to build each area. Adjust fill tolerance and edge expansion to avoid gaps along the ink. Add a layer mask where the boundary needs further editing.

## Prepare for painting
Check the silhouette with the ink hidden. Use clipping for paint above a base shape, masks for editable boundaries, or alpha lock to repaint existing pixels.

## Sketch the idea
Use a pencil for gesture, proportion, and composition. Work small enough to judge the whole image before adding detail.

## Block in the colors
On a separate layer, place a few large color shapes. Use a broad brush, lasso, or fill to separate the subject from the background and check the light and dark values.

## Adjust the composition
Move, scale, or rotate selected parts. Mirror the canvas view to check the drawing, then keep the sketch and color blocks separate for inking.

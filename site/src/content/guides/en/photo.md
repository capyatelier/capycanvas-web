You edit one photo in three stages in the [Photo](/docs/start/workspaces/)
workspace. Use a photo of your own. The example shows a terrarium with a lava
rock and a fern on a black background.

![The Photo workspace with the edited terrarium photo on the canvas and its layers in the Layers panel.](shot:photo/overview)

## The Photo workspace

Select **Photo** in the workspace switcher in the title bar. The tutorial uses
these parts of the workspace:

- The Tools toolbar at the left, with **Crop**, the selection tools, and the healing tools.
- **Tool Options** at the top, with the settings of the current tool.
- The **Properties** and **Layers** panels in the right column.

## The stages

1. [Opening and cropping](/docs/photo/crop/): the photo opened and cropped to 5:4.
2. [Retouching](/docs/photo/retouch/): dust and a smudge removed on a layer above the photo.
3. [Adjusting and exporting](/docs/photo/adjust/): **Curves**, **Vibrance**, and a local **Shadows/Highlights**, then a `.capy` file and a JPEG for the web.

## The finished layers

The edited photo has these layers, from top to bottom:

| Layer | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Shadows/Highlights** | Lifts the shadows in the rock, masked to a feathered selection |
| **Vibrance** | Raises the vibrance of the whole photo |
| **Curves** | An S curve for the whole photo |
| *Retouch* | Healing strokes |
| The photo layer | The photo, named after its file |
| **Paper** | Hidden |

Nothing is painted on the photo layer.

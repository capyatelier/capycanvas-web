We redesigned the [dry media simulation engine](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/commit/beac20d0073f9d1311cd359fac6e5ac12d050909) from scratch to accurately simulate pencils, charcoal, chalk, pastels, and crayons. Pen pressure and tilt affect stroke shape and coverage, with separate controls for material hardness, paper texture response, and stroke opacity.

This release also adds [built-in paper types](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/commit/66c2dc03325688f73422824b0f01d1de3c2eebb3), including Bristol, hot and cold press watercolor paper, sanded paper, washi, canvas, and linen. You can import your own paper textures, change their scale and lighting, and control how much texture shows through paint. Native app exports include Paper lighting.

![Charcoal on Unryu, Pointy Pencil on Handmade, Pastel Block on Sanded, and Crayon on Linen.](https://capycanvas.art/assets/releases/1.0.13/dry-media.webp)

Other changes:

- [Expanded GPU support](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/commit/15f01f80e92b49475f3ba5cfd6f3237abb21a8d7), fixing canvas startup on devices including the iPad mini 6. The [native iPad app now supports iPadOS 17.5+](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/commit/50872c7eba078673c2e026eccdfe3e49c95e1678). The web editor still requires iPadOS 26+.
- [Native Windows Arm64 builds](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/commit/97f7a4528177693863ecc16ba3846b4d67292a23).
- [Pen pressure calibration](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/commit/3332d9f90f2b5e7d6265b057f16fe66dd7e0d6e9) and [per-brush stroke smoothing](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/commit/cbc74b304ad0764556a2bf66731061ce3c9f1570).
- A redesigned [New drawing dialog](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/commit/c89bd5bc0c0da8bd1b0cd0bfb680c387b677a670) with physical units, paper choices, and searchable presets.
- A combined [Layers and Properties drawer](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/commit/e5c2e8574971bceaaccb92a887fc801576569476) in Sketch.

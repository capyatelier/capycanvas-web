There's a lot in this release - over 1,600 commits since the last one. Capy Canvas now works on phones, and is a lot faster and more reliable than before. The top feedback on 1.0.15 was that it was slow, especially on Windows and low-RAM devices, so that was the focus.

## Phones

Capy Canvas has a new UI tailored for small screens ([#15](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/issues/15)). Now you can draw on the go, directly on your iPhone and Android.

![Capy Canvas on a phone in Photo, a tablet in Sketch and a desktop in Paint.](https://capycanvas.art/assets/releases/1.0.16/devices.webp)

## Speed

Startup is up to 5x faster, because Capy Canvas now compiles fewer shaders when it starts and reuses them between launches.

We also built a more responsive display stack that loads textures at your screen's native resolution and keeps large images on disk instead of in RAM. Photos open up to 5x faster, and Capy Canvas now supports images up to 1 gigapixel.

## Windows

On Windows, Capy Canvas starts up to 4x faster ([#21](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/issues/21)) and has better multi-GPU and graphics tablet support ([#32](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/issues/32)).

## Line art

We redesigned smoothing to account for pen pressure, and added a new taper engine that works with or without a pressure-sensitive pen.

![Ink strokes drawn with a Proportional taper, next to the Taper settings.](https://capycanvas.art/assets/releases/1.0.16/taper.webp)

## Other changes

- No more waiting for brushes to load when you switch drawings ([#30](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/issues/30)).
- The bucket tool closes small gaps in line art and leaves no white edge along the lines.
- The new **Edit ▸ Flat All Areas** command fills every closed area of your line art at once, on its own layer.
- Shortcuts now work with non-Latin keyboard layouts like Cyrillic, Greek and Arabic ([#26](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/pull/26)).
- Workspaces, palettes and brush settings now carry over between updates.

[Full changelog](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/compare/v1.0.15...v1.0.16)

## Before you update

As usual, please back up your artwork before upgrading. Drawings saved in 1.0.15 or later will keep opening in future versions, but until we've had a few stable releases, it's better to have a copy.

There's a lot in this release - over 1,600 commits since the last one. Capy Canvas now works on phones, and is a lot faster and more reliable than before. The top feedback on 1.0.15 was that it was slow, especially on Windows and low-RAM devices, so that's where most of the work went.

## Phones

Capy Canvas has a new UI tailored for small screens ([#15](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/issues/15)). Draw with pen or finger on iPhone and Android, or in your phone's browser.

![Capy Canvas on a phone in Photo, a tablet in Sketch and a desktop in Paint.](https://capycanvas.art/assets/releases/1.0.16/devices.webp)

## Speed

Startup is up to 5x faster, because Capy Canvas now compiles fewer shaders when it starts and reuses them between launches.

We also built a new display stack. Photos open up to 5x faster, because it loads textures at your screen's native resolution. It's also more responsive, and it uses disk instead of RAM for large images. Capy Canvas now supports images up to 1 gigapixel.

## Windows

Capy Canvas starts up to 4x faster on Windows ([#21](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/issues/21)). It also handles multi-GPU systems and graphics tablets better ([#32](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/issues/32)).

## Line art

We redesigned smoothing and added a new taper engine for pens. Pen pressure is now smoothed too, and tapers work even if your pen has no pressure.

![Ink strokes drawn with a Proportional taper, next to the Taper settings.](https://capycanvas.art/assets/releases/1.0.16/taper.webp)

## Other changes

- No more waiting for brushes to load when you switch drawings ([#30](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/issues/30)).
- Fill closes small gaps in line art and fills under the lines.
- Flat All Areas fills every closed area onto a new layer.
- Shortcuts now work with non-Latin keyboard layouts like Cyrillic, Greek and Arabic ([#26](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/pull/26)).
- Workspaces, palettes and brush settings now carry over between updates.

[Full changelog](https://github.com/capyatelier/capycanvas/compare/v1.0.15...v1.0.16)

## Before you update

As usual, please back up your artwork before upgrading. The .capy format is stable, but it's better not to take chances until we have a few stable releases under our belt.

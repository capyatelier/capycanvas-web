## Vector layers

We now support vector drawing alongside raster painting, so you can get 100% crisp lines at any zoom level.

![Abstract vector artwork with a selected path and its curve handles.](https://capycanvas.art/assets/releases/1.0.15/vector-layers.webp)

## Tool bubbles

To help folks on small-screen devices, we are rolling out a new way to interact with tools. In **Sketch** mode, just drag and drop any tool in the canvas to have it close at hand.

![Tool bubbles over an abstract painting.](https://capycanvas.art/assets/releases/1.0.15/tool-bubbles.webp)

## Blenders

We did a complete rewrite of our blending engine, adding a total of 18 new blenders, ranging from simulated physical media blenders that respect the texture of the paper, paint simulation blenders, as well as a few crazy looking ones for digital art.

![Bristle Blender, Dither Blender, and Marbling applied to shaded strokes. The left end of each stroke shows the original paint.](https://capycanvas.art/assets/releases/1.0.15/blenders.webp)

## Liquify

A full rewrite of the Liquify engine improves numerical stability in order to help keep lines and textures crisp when being liquified. This rewrite also enables a few new Liquify modes - **Edge** and **Reconstruct**.

## Pen tilt calibration

Because pen tilt feels different on each device (and because many of the cheaper Android tablets actually report corrupted pen tilt data), we release a new pen tilt calibration UI that lets you change the tilt curve, sensitivity, and axis mapping so that tilt feels right for you on every device.

## Other changes

- Rotation now snaps to 0°, 90°, 180°, and 270° by default.
- Canvas action bars like for the selection and transform tools, have been entirely redesigned for ease of use.
